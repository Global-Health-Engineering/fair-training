Idea

- We train 15 ETH participants over 12 months to make their research data FAIR (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable) using hands-on work with their own data.
- The group would be a mix of 1-2 Professors, 2-3 Senior Scientists / Post Docs, 7 PhD students, 2-3 Scientific Assistants / Technical Administrative staff
- The programme is built around 5 workshops with a total of 28 hours of workshop time
- Alongside the workshops we would run bi-monthly AI playground events (already ongoing at GHE and open to anyone) and quarterly 2-hour hands-on project team meetings.

Budget

The total programme cost is 49,400 CHF, covered by a program fee of 2,000 CHF per participant (15 × 2,000 = 30,000 CHF) plus the following:

- Organisational Change for Open Science (Greg Wilson): 6,500 CHF (honorarium, travel, accommodation, Villa Hatt)
- Workshops (catering and Villa Hatt): 3,900 CHF
- Software (Claude Code Pro, 15 seats × 6 months): 9,000 CHF

The 30,000 CHF program fee goes to GHE as payment for delivering the training. The financial flow could be simplified if GHE receives the money directly from the programme rather than through each participant's group.
