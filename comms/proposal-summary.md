# FAIR by doing: hands-on FAIR training for ETH researchers

**In one line:** A 12-month programme that trains 15 ETH researchers to make their own research data FAIR by doing it, taking each person from a raw file to a published, citable data package.

> Summary of the FAIR Competence Funding (2nd call) proposal, for sharing with stakeholders. Tracks the current draft; open items are still being closed before the 15 September 2026 due date.

## The basics

- **Call:** ETH FAIR Competence Funding, 2nd call. Due 15 September 2026.
- **Applicants:** Lars Schöbitz (corresponding) and Adriana Clavijo, Global Health Engineering, D-MAVT.
- **Budget requested:** CHF 49,400 (under the CHF 50,000 cap), all direct cost, no salary line.
- **Duration:** 12 months, starting January or February 2027.

## Who it is for

- 15 ETH researchers who already hold, or are moving into, research data management responsibility.
- Mixed cohort by design: 1-2 professors, 2-3 senior scientists/postdocs, 7 doctoral students, 2-3 scientific or technical staff.
- Drawn from several departments, so FAIR practice can be studied across disciplines and data types.

## What participants do

- Each brings a real dataset and takes it to a published, FAIR, citable data package.
- They then apply the same workflow to the rest of their data, with ongoing support.

## Programme: 5 workshops, 28 hours

- 2 x 4h: FAIR data sharing and data management (the openwashdata workflow, on participants' own data).
- 1 x 4h: Git and GitHub/GitLab collaborative workflows.
- 1 x 4h: GitHub/GitLab for task and project management.
- 1 x 8h: agentic AI workflows for data management.
- 1 full day: organisational change for open science (Greg Wilson, at Villa Hatt).
- Plus: AI Playground sessions (three per term, open to all), quarterly hands-on team meetings, a Matrix support channel, and 1:1 support.

## What makes it different (vs the DSN and RDM Summer School)

- Hands-on and practical, not theory. Participants work on their own real data.
- Aimed at people who already carry RDM responsibility, not novices.
- Addresses community-specific FAIR requirements that introductory offerings do not reach.
- Runs a year-long thread on how to declare AI use in research (tied to the GFRN AI Disclosure Working Group).
- Complements the ETH Library offerings and feeds lessons back to the DSN, rather than duplicating them.

## What it produces

- 15 published, FAIR, citable data packages from 15 ETH researchers.
- 15 researchers able to repeat the workflow and pass it to their groups.
- A reusable programme GHE can run for future cohorts.
- A research output: findings on how FAIR practice, data security, and AI-use disclosure differ across communities.
- All teaching material published as Open Educational Resources.
