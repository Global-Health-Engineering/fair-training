# Expression of interest form: FAIR by doing

The Google Form collects expressions of interest from the email campaign (see `recruitment-plan.md`). It is deliberately short, one section with eleven questions, so that people fill it in on the spot. It gives enough to check the employment criterion and to build the cohort mix by role, department, and research approach. Dataset details, tools and coding experience, and the availability questions are asked in a second round with the selected people once the funding decision is known.

The responses hold personal data. Keep the response sheet out of git. Only this blank specification belongs in the repository. Draft date: 2026-09-03.

## Form settings

- Title: FAIR by doing: expression of interest
- Description (as entered in the form on 2026-09-03): "A twelve-month, hands-on programme (early 2027 to early 2028) that helps ETH researchers make their own research data FAIR in the era of agentic AI tools. We, Adriana and Lars of the Global Health Engineering group, are applying for ETH FAIR Competence Funding and we are looking for fifteen named participants in the proposal by 15 September 2026."
- Leave "Collect email addresses" and "Limit to 1 response" off. Both need a Google account, and ETH accounts are Microsoft accounts. Use response validation on the email field instead.

## Questions

1. Institutional email address. Short answer, required. Response validation: text contains "ethz.ch".
2. First name. Short answer, required.
3. Surname. Short answer, required.
4. Department. Dropdown, required. Options: D-ARCH, D-BAUG, D-BIOL, D-BSSE, D-CHAB, D-EAPS, D-GESS, D-HEST, D-INFK, D-ITET, D-MATH, D-MATL, D-MAVT, D-MTEC, D-PHYS, D-USYS, Central unit (ETH Library, IT Services, other), Other.
5. Research group or institute. Short answer, required.
6. Current role. Multiple choice, required. Options: Professor; Senior scientist or group leader; Postdoc; Doctoral student; Scientific or research assistant; Technical or administrative staff (e.g., lab manager, data manager); Other.
7. Do you expect to be employed at ETH until at least the end of 2027? Multiple choice, required. Options: Yes; Probably; No.
8. Field of research, in a few words. Short answer, required.
9. Which research approaches do you mainly use? Checkboxes, required. Options: Quantitative (experiments, measurements, sensors); Surveys; Qualitative (interviews, focus groups, documents); Computational or simulation; Mixed methods.
10. What is your motivation for participation? Paragraph, required.
11. May we name you in the proposal as a committed participant? Multiple choice, required. Options: Yes; I need to check with my supervisor first; Only once the funding is confirmed.

## Using the answers for selection

- Hard criterion: question 7 is "Yes" or "Probably".
- Cohort quotas, from the proposal: question 6 fills one to two professors, two to three senior scientists or postdocs, seven doctoral students, and two to three scientific or technical staff. If the senior places do not fill, take more doctoral students.
- Mix, from the community-specific angle: spread question 4 (department), question 8 (field), and question 9 (approach) as widely as possible, so the cohort covers quantitative and qualitative research.
- Conversion: question 11 tells you who can go on the commitment sheet now and who needs a supervisor conversation first.
- Questions 1 to 6 give the name, department, role, and email columns of the commitment sheet. The dataset column comes from the second round.

## Second round (after the funding decision)

Ask the selected participants for the dataset they bring (description, data type, personal or confidential content, whether it can be published), their tools and coding experience (languages, the four-rank coding scale, editors, Git, AI tools), and their availability.
