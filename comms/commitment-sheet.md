## What each participant commits to

By signing, the participant confirms that they:

- are employed at ETH Zurich and expect to remain so for the programme duration (about twelve months from early 2027),
- will take part in the twelve-month FAIR by doing programme (five workshops totalling 28 hours, plus support sessions and community events),
- will bring one of their own research datasets to work on during the programme, and
- understand that their place is funded through the ETH FAIR Competence Funding, subject to the grant being approved.
