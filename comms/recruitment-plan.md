# Recruitment and communication plan: FAIR by doing

Goal: fifteen ETH researchers signed up with a commitment sheet before the proposal is submitted on 15 September 2026, 17:00 CET. The signed list is a submission attachment and it is the strongest evidence for section 2.5 (a real, named target community).

Draft date: 2026-09-01, emails revised 2026-09-03. Owner: Lars Schöbitz, with Adriana Clavijo Daza.

## The constraint

There are about two weeks to the due date. Fifteen signed commitments in two weeks is tight, so the plan sends an expression-of-interest teaser this week, before the proposal is fully polished, and converts interest into signatures on a rolling basis. A soft "expression of interest" ask is easier to say yes to than "sign here", so the teaser asks for interest first and the commitment sheet follows.

## The cohort we are recruiting for

Fifteen places, mixed on purpose so the programme can show how FAIR practice differs by community and data type:

- 1 to 2 professors
- 2 to 3 senior scientists or postdocs
- 7 doctoral students
- 2 to 3 scientific or technical staff

If the senior places do not fill, take more doctoral students. Each participant must bring a real dataset they can work on and, in principle, share.

Entry expectations (state these in the teaser so people self-select):

- Employed at ETH for the project duration.
- A dataset from their own work that can be made open, or worked toward open.
- Some experience with a programming language for data analysis (R, Python, Julia, JavaScript, Stata, SPSS, C++, or any other scripting language). Not a complete beginner, but not an expert either. E.g. (rank 1: "has written lines of code for their own use" on a 4-rank scale)
- Some experience with a programming IDE (VS Code, RStudio, Positron, or similar).
- Helpful but not required: previous ETH Library RDM Summer School attendance.

## Message: what we say

Keep the pitch to the benefit for the participant, then the ask.

- What they get: over twelve months, hands-on support to turn their own dataset into a published, citable FAIR data package with a DOI, plus practical skills in Git, GitHub or GitLab, project management, and agentic AI workflows for data. They finish with a public, citable output and a repeatable workflow for the rest of their data.
- Why now: the programme is funded through the ETH FAIR Competence Funding, so the place is paid for (subject to the grant). We need their name on a commitment sheet by mid-September to include them in the proposal.
- The ask: reply with an expression of interest this week; sign the one-page commitment sheet when we send it.

## Channels: where we send it, in order

1.  Direct, named asks first (highest conversion). Lars and Adriana each write a short personal list of ETH researchers who fit the cohort and have shareable data, and message them directly. Aim for a list of 25 to 30 names to land 15 commitments. This is the main engine; do it first and now.
2.  Global Health Engineering networks. The AI playground community (already active and open to ETH and beyond) and the openwashdata contacts at ETH. These people already know the workflow, so they convert fast.
3.  The ETH Data Stewardship Network. Adriana is a member; a short note to the DSN reaches data-minded researchers across departments and signals the DSN link the call rewards. [TODO check DSN posting norms before sending.]
4.  Department and group mailing lists for the target departments, once the teaser text is agreed. Broader reach, lower conversion; use to top up.
5.  A short post on the GHE channels and Lars's LinkedIn if places remain after the direct asks. [TODO decide whether a public post is wanted this early.]

Sequence the channels: direct asks and GHE networks in the first few days, DSN and mailing lists mid-week, public post only if needed.

## Timeline to 15 September

- Days 1 to 3 (this week): finalise the teaser text, build the named-contact lists, send direct asks and post to GHE networks. Open the commitment sheet.
- Days 4 to 6: send to the DSN and target department or group lists. Start converting expressions of interest into signatures.
- Days 7 to 10: follow up with anyone who expressed interest but has not signed. Fill remaining places from the reserve list of doctoral students.
- Days 11 to 13: close the list at fifteen signed, lock the commitment sheet, and attach it to the proposal.
- 15 September, before 17:00 CET: submit.
